Our Story

The Urban Spoon began in 2015 as a small corner cafe. Today it is a full restaurant, but our approach has not changed: simple recipes, honest ingredients and friendly service.

Our mission is to make every meal feel like a good moment worth remembering.

Our Values

What guides our kitchen and our team.

Fresh Daily

Menus change with the season so ingredients are at their best.

Local First

We buy vegetables, dairy and eggs from nearby farms.

Made With Care

Sauces, bread and desserts are made in our own kitchen.
